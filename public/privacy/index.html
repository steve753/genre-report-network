Privacy Policy

Genre Report Network · reports.stevepieper.com · Last updated September 3, 2026

This policy covers the genre-report publications at reports.stevepieper.com — Thriller Monthly, Fantasy Quarterly, Romance Quarterly, Mystery Quarterly, Science Fiction Quarterly, and Horror Quarterly — published by Polymath Consulting & Publishing Inc. It's written to be read, not skimmed past: it says exactly what we collect, exactly what we do with it, and exactly what we don't do.

What we collect

When you subscribe, we collect your email address, an optional first name if you choose to give one, and a consent record: which page you subscribed from, when, and any campaign tags (UTM parameters) in the page address that brought you there. When you interact with our subscription system, our server also sees your IP address and browser user-agent string, as every web server does. We don't run analytics scripts, and the reports themselves set no tracking cookies of our own.

What we do with it

We use your email address to send you the publication you subscribed to — one email per issue, after you've confirmed your address (double opt-in), each with a one-click unsubscribe that is honored immediately. We use your first name, if you gave one, to address you by it.

The advertising-measurement part, stated plainly: we advertise these reports on Meta (Facebook and Instagram). To measure whether that advertising works, when you subscribe or confirm we send Meta a "Lead" or "CompleteRegistration" event through Meta's Conversions API. That event includes a hashed (one-way encrypted, SHA-256) copy of your email address — Meta can match it against its own records but cannot read the address from the hash — along with your IP address, user-agent, and Meta's own browser identifiers if its cookies are present from a click on one of our ads. This is measurement, not enrichment: it tells us which ads produced subscribers. Meta's handling of this data is governed by Meta's privacy policy.

What we don't do

We never sell your information. We never rent or trade our list. We send no third-party offers to it. Apart from the Meta measurement events described above and the service providers below, your information goes to no one.

Who processes data for us

Subscriber records are stored with Supabase (hosted database). Emails are delivered through Postmark. The site is served by Cloudflare. Each acts as a service provider processing data on our instructions.

Retention and your choices

We keep your record while you're subscribed. When you unsubscribe, we stop emailing you immediately and keep a minimal suppression record so we never email you again by mistake. To access, correct, or delete your information entirely, email reports@stevepieper.com and we'll handle it directly.

Scope

These publications are written for and offered to a United States audience. If we open subscriptions to readers in regions with additional consent requirements, we'll add the required consent mechanisms first, not after.
